Picking

Each market opens a bottom-up picker with a search box and club crests. Clubs are ordered favourites-first; the ~500 PL players are alphabetical — type "hal" for Haaland, "amo" for Amorim. Everything auto-saves as you go — no big Submit button.

Who picked who?

Private until lock, public after. Before Fri 21 Aug, only you see your picks — nobody's copying anyone. Once the lock fires, each market gets a 👥 N picks chip that opens up every fella's card + a top-3 consensus strip (🥇🥈🥉). All season, the debates keep running.

How to play — 30 seconds

  1. Open the app → tap 🎯 Predictor on the bottom nav.
  2. Two tabs at the top: Match of the Week (default) and Season Card. World Cup archive lives quietly to the right.
  3. MoW: tap +/− to set both scores, Submit. Back to you next Monday.
  4. Season Card: tap each of the 7 markets, use the search-y pickers, done. Bring it back before Fri 21 Aug 19:00.
  5. Notifications opt-in on the Tonight tab if you haven't already — that's how you'll hear when picks open, lock, and settle.
