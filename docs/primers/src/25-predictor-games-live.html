Match of the Week

Every Monday morning, the app auto-picks a marquee fixture from the coming weekend. It weights the pick by favourite_club affinity — a Millwall fan's derby beats a random 3pm kickoff — with a "recently-picked" penalty so West Ham fixtures don't dominate every week. You pick a scoreline; it locks at kickoff.

Season Card

The season-long predictor. Seven markets, one form, fill it in once. The card locks 1h before the PL opener on Fri 21 Aug 19:00 UK, then reopens for a 2-day edit window on 2-4 Sept so you can react to the summer transfer window. After that, locked for the year.

Ordered slots: 3 pts if the exact team is in the exact slot, 1 pt if you had the right team in the wrong slot. Max 72 pts for a clean-sheet season.

🏆 Grand-slam bonus: +25 pts if you somehow get every market at max points. Silly-hard, but the reward is there. Season ceiling: 97 pts.

How you pick

  • Each market opens a bottom-sheet picker — searchable, shows club crests, favourites-first for clubs, alphabetical + type-to-search for the ~500 PL players.
  • Auto-saves as you go. No big Submit button — every pick writes immediately.
  • Header shows a live countdown to lock and a running N/13 picked tally so you can see how complete your card is.

Who picked who?

Private until lock, public after. Before Fri 21 Aug, only you see your picks — nobody's copying anyone. Once the lock fires, each market shows a 👥 N picks chip that opens a bottom-sheet with every fella's pick + a consensus strip (🥇🥈🥉 top 3 most-backed options). All season, the debates keep running.

How to play — 30 seconds

  1. Open the Wanstead Fellas app → tap 🎯 Predictor on the bottom nav.
  2. Two tabs at the top: Match of the Week (default) and Season Card. World Cup archive lives to the right of them.
  3. MoW: tap +/− to set both scores, hit Submit. Done in 10 seconds. Comes back to you next Monday.
  4. Season Card: tap each of the 7 markets, use the searchable pickers. Auto-saves as you go. Bring it back before Fri 21 Aug 19:00.
  5. Notifications opt-in on the Tonight tab if you haven't already — that's how you'll hear when picks open, lock, and settle.
