Welcome. This is everything you need to know to use the app in five minutes flat.

Getting In

  1. Open the app link your captain sent you on your phone.
  2. Tap Sign up and use your email + a password.
  3. Confirm your name when prompted — that's it, you're in.
Install it on your home screen so it behaves like a proper app:
  • iPhone (Safari): tap the share icon → Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): tap the menu → Install app / Add to Home Screen.
You'll get an app icon, full-screen experience, and faster loads. You only sign in once.

What To Actually Do, Week By Week

Wednesday — sign up by 10pm

  • Open Next Game → tap the big green I'm In button.
  • Changed your mind? Tap Drop Out before 10pm Wednesday. After 10pm sign-ups are locked.
  • Full already? You'll go to the Reserves list. If anyone drops, you get auto-promoted in priority order (paid casuals first, then by sign-up time).

Thursday afternoon

  • Open Teams to see your line-up and team colours. The teams are auto-balanced by the captain's tool.

Thursday night

  • Match tab shows the score live as the captain enters it.
  • Cast votes in the Cast Your Votes card on the Match tab:
    • 🏆 Man of the Match — best player on the night.
    • 🤡 Dick of the Day — the lighter award.
  • Voting closes Friday 9am. Winners auto-publish.

Friday morning

  • Match tab shows the full report, scorers, MOTM/DOTD winners and league table for the night.
  • Stats (under More) ticks up: top scorers, appearances, distance covered, fines.

Common How-Tos

How do I see my player card?

More → My Profile (or tap your avatar at the top of any screen). You get your stats, ratings, badges and a button to add Match Fitness (Polar / Garmin / Strava GPX upload — see below).

How do I add my run data after a match?

On your player card, tap + Add match fitness. You can either upload a .gpx or .tcx file exported from your watch app, or type the numbers in by hand. Re-uploading for the same date replaces the previous entry.

How do I manage my kid's account?

If you've asked the admin to link your child to your account, you'll see them on the Next Game page with their own In/Out button below yours. Tap In to sign them up; Tap Out to drop them.

I dropped out — what happens?

The first person on the reserves list gets auto-bumped into your slot. They'll see it next time they open the app.

Where do I see how much I owe?

More → My Finances — running tally of fines + casual game payments. Mark them paid once you've sorted with the admin.

During The World Cup

A 🏆 World Cup tab appears at the bottom of the app. It has four sub-tabs:

Sub-tabWhat's there
HubUpcoming fixtures (today + tomorrow) with picks, plus the leaderboard preview.
LeagueFull predictor standings. % is your hit-rate across all settled matches.
My PicksYour own scorecard, what you've predicted and what's still locked.
SweepThe £240 paper sweepstake digitised — pot, prizes, ownership grid, current leaders. Your teams highlighted in cyan.
Picks lock 5 minutes before kick-off. Miss it and that match counts as a wrong pick — the leaderboard denominator is total settled matches, not just the ones you predicted.

House Rules Baked Into The App

  • Sign-up cap: 32 confirmed per Thursday (sometimes 36 for special weeks). Extras go to the reserves list.
  • Priority order when bumping for a slot: subscribed > paid casuals (wtp_priority) > casuals (wtp).
  • Late drops: if someone drops late, the highest-priority reserve gets pulled in automatically.
  • Voting closes 9am Friday. No late votes.

When Something Looks Wrong

  • Force-refresh first. On iPhone: swipe up to close the app, then re-open. On desktop: Cmd+Shift+R / Ctrl+Shift+R. Most "weird" data is a cached old view.
  • Still wrong? Drop the admin a message — pabarham@gmail.com or in the WhatsApp group. Include a screenshot and what you were doing.
