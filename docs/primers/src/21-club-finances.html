Where it lives

Everything is on the new Club tab inside Admin (between Finance and Families). Three sections stacked:

  • Balance callout — the live pot, computed on the fly from every source below.
  • Subscriptions — one row per subscribed player for the 2026-27 season, with a Mark paid toggle. All subs are already ticked off — nothing outstanding.
  • Expenses by month — every pitch invoice (Apr–Jul paid, Aug–Mar pre-seeded unpaid), the pre-season footballs, and an Add expense button for ad-hoc items (equipment, food, tournament, other).

Why "folds to bank pot" matters. Before this feature, cash was tracked in a mix of spreadsheets and my head. Now the app is the source of truth — every credit and debit on the Wanstead Fellas Treasury statement maps to a row inside the app, and any drift shows up on this tab straight away.

The reconciliation story

Rolling every bank line back to a specific app row uncovered £331 of income that had no home — a one-off catch-all that now sits as a single club_income row dated 1 Apr 2026. Composed of:

  • Overpayments bundled with subs (Davies +£65, Sherman +£40, Perrie +£25, etc.)
  • Past-season settlements paid after 1 Apr — labelled May/Jun payments not tied to specific WTP rows
  • Josh Edwards' £10 sub discount for his 2 pre-app April WTPs (why the bank shows £85 not £95)
  • Off-app spreadsheet fines cleared before the auto-fine flow shipped

Going forward this number should stay flat or shrink — every new bank credit lands via the app first. If we ever want per-player granularity restored, we walk each May/Jun bank line back to a specific WTP row and the catch-all decreases accordingly.
