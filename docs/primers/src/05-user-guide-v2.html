Quick start

1 Open the app link your captain sent you. Tap Sign up → email + password. Confirm your name when prompted. That's it — you're in.

2 Install it on your home screen so it behaves like a proper app:

  • 📱 iPhone (Safari): tap the share icon → Add to Home Screen.
  • 🤖 Android (Chrome): tap the menu → Install app / Add to Home Screen.

3 Pick your position. The app will nudge you the first time you visit Next Game — tap your spot (🧤 GK · 🛡️ DEF · ⚙️ MID · ⚽ ATT). Helps the auto-balancer build fairer teams.

House rules baked into the app
  • Cap: 32 confirmed per Thursday (sometimes 36 for special weeks). Extras go to reserves.
  • Priority order when bumping for a slot: subscribed > paid casuals (WTP+) > casuals (WTP).
  • Late drops: if someone drops late, the highest-priority reserve gets pulled in automatically.
  • Voting closes 10am Friday. No late votes.

Common how-tos

🆘 The match is full — what happens?

You'll join the reserves. The list is auto-ranked by tier (subscribed first, then WTP+, then WTP by sign-up time). If anyone drops out, the top reserve gets bumped into the slot — no admin needed.

👶 I want to sign up my kid

If admin has linked your child to your account, you'll see them on Next Game as a small pill next to your I'm In button. Tap it to sign them up; tap again to drop them.

🚪 I need to drop out

Tap your green I'm In button → confirm. If anyone's waiting, your slot goes to the top reserve automatically. You can change your mind later but you may not get your spot back.

🔒 What if I miss the 10pm deadline?

Sign-ups are locked. The captain can still slot you in via admin tools if there's room, but message in WhatsApp first.

👀 Why can't I see "Not In"?

The "Not In · 59" list is admin-only — keeps the page clean for everyone else.

The status of your sign-up is always written on the big button:
✓ I'm In · 🟡 On the Waiting List · ⏳ Reserve (was confirmed, got bumped)

Your Profile

Open via More → My Profile or tap your avatar at the top of any screen. You'll find:

  • Your Top Trumps card — overall rating + six attributes (pace, shooting, passing, dribbling, defence, physicality) or GK stats if you're a keeper. Updates as the season goes on.
  • Preferred position — pick a primary and an optional backup. Used by the team balancer (so every team gets a striker, a defender and a GK) and feeds future position-aware leaderboards.
  • Photo, name, age group, favourite club — set once, easy to change.
  • Match fitness — add post-match GPS data (see below).
  • My Finances — your running balance.

Position picker

Four buttons. Tap once for primary, tap a second to set a backup, tap again to clear. Helpful if you're "happy at MID, can play DEF in a pinch."

🧤 GK 🛡️ DEF ⚙️ MID ⚽ ATT

📈 Add match fitness

On your player card, tap + Add match fitness. Either upload a .gpx or .tcx file exported from your watch app (Polar, Garmin, Strava), or type the numbers in by hand. Re-uploading for the same date replaces the previous entry.

💷 My Finances

Open via More → My Finances. Two sections:

  • WTP Games This Month — for casual players, a £5 fee is now added automatically whenever you sign up confirmed for a Thursday. The panel shows count + £ total + per-game lines.
  • Outstanding Fines — any lateness / drop-out / cuntiness charges, with date and amount.
Big number at the top tells you what you owe. "All clear ✓" means you're paid up.

🏆 World Cup tab

Appears at the bottom of the app during tournaments. Four sub-tabs:

HubUpcoming fixtures (today + tomorrow) with your picks · leaderboard preview.
LeagueFull predictor standings. % is your hit rate across all settled matches.
My PicksYour scorecard — what you predicted, what's still locked.
SweepThe £240 paper sweepstake digitised — pot, prizes, ownership grid, leaders for Most Conceded and Most Reds. Your teams highlighted in cyan at the bottom.
Picks lock 5 minutes before kick-off. Miss it and that match counts as a wrong pick — the leaderboard denominator is total settled matches, not just the ones you predicted. Set a phone reminder for early-morning games.

🛟 When something looks wrong

  1. Force-refresh first. iPhone: swipe up to close the app, then re-open. Desktop: Cmd+Shift+R / Ctrl+Shift+R. Most "weird" data is a cached old view.
  2. Check your phone has signal — most live features need it.
  3. Still wrong? Drop the admin a message: pabarham@gmail.com or in the WhatsApp group. Include a screenshot and what you were doing.
