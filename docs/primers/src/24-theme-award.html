What "theme" means each week

Admin sets a free-text prompt on the Teams tab when publishing the sides — one line, memorable, room for interpretation. A few for the feel:

  • Kevin Keegan tribute — bubble perm, one-touch passing, closing eyes on volleys
  • Peak dad-strength — the deceptive-shoulder, hooked-boot, refuses-to-die tackle era
  • Most Ronaldinho moment — a piece of skill, or the smile that goes with it
  • Best celebration — the aeroplane, the sliding knee, the awkward finger-guns
  • Grimsby Town circa 2003 — a beautifully bad chip, ideally scored anyway

You'll see the prompt in three places from the moment the teams are published: the "teams ready" push, the Tonight tab strip, and the ballot itself once voting opens.

The flow, front to back

Wed evening
Admin sets the theme prompt in the Teams tab, then publishes.
Publish moment
Group gets the "teams are ready" push — theme embedded so it's live in the WhatsApp banter immediately.
Thu 8:00pm
Players arrive at the pitch already knowing what they're being scored on — improv, tribute, dad-mode, whatever it is.
Thu 10:30pm
Voting opens — the ballot on the Match tab now has three slots per player. Cast all three.
Fri 10:00am
Results publish — the awards ping goes out with all three winners; theme winner joins MOTM/DOTD in the record.

Rules

  • Same as MOTM/DOTD — one vote per player, editable until 10am Fri.
  • You can vote for yourself. Whether you should is a moral question.
  • Ties are shared — if two players tie the vote, both take the award (like MOTM).
  • Admin can override the auto-tally same way they can for MOTM/DOTD (the "ADMIN CALL" pill). Reserve for edge cases.
  • Opt-in per match — if admin skips the prompt one week (nothing came to mind, or the vibe wasn't right), the whole slot disappears. No pressure to always have a theme.

Where the "theme ideas" come from

Two channels for now:

  • Admin picks it — usually decided Wed evening at team-publish time, sometimes revised on Thu if a better idea lands.
  • Group suggests it — nominations in the WhatsApp thread welcomed. If yours gets picked, expect banter.
Coming later. A "Theme history" section on Stats (past prompts + winners), a "Theme Chameleon" badge for anyone who wins 3+, and an in-app suggestion form so the group can drop ideas without cluttering the WhatsApp chat.
