MOTM / DOTD · Engagement Review
P601 · ANALYSIS
Brief (19 Jun 2026): "MOTM and DOTD voting works but engagement isn't high. Looking at the UX, it feels sub-optimal. Review the workflow, layout and provide suggested improvements that will maximise engagement." This is the diagnosis that drove the June 2026 voting rework.

Two distinct leaks

Leak 1 · Acquisition
~Half the squad never votes at all
Average turnout sits at ~44%. The other half don't engage with either award — they're never in the funnel.
Leak 2 · Drop-off
Quarter-to-half of MOTM voters bail before DOTD
28 May: 15 → 8. The second award, sitting below the first, lost people to scroll fatigue. Two awards × ~32 players ≈ 70 chips on screen.

Why — structural causes

  • Overnight window. Voting opened 22:00 match night, closed 09:00. Most of it while people are asleep. Miss it Thursday night → missed it.
  • No reminder of any kind. No push, no nudge. A voter had to remember on their own, open the app, navigate to Match.
  • High voting effort. Each award rendered all 32–36 players as an alphabetical wall of chips, no search, no grouping. Directly explains the DOTD drop-off.
  • No payoff loop. Results only appeared after close, with nothing signalling they were in. You voted into a void.
Key insight: the match report is fully decoupled from voting — it publishes the instant an admin saves the result. So widening the voting window never delays the report; only the award reveal moves. This unlocks the biggest single lever at zero cost to the rest of the night.

Prioritised levers

By impact ÷ effort. Shipped highlighted in green, partial in yellow, still open in cyan.

1
Widen / re-time the window PARTIAL
Biggest single lever, tiny change. Two independent knobs — open time (earlier = pure win, zero report cost) and close time (only delays reveal). Squad chose to keep it tight (close 10:00) so awards still land Friday morning.
2
Add a reminder OPEN
Highest impact, highest effort. PWA scaffolding (manifest + service worker) exists, so web-push is feasible. Cheaper interim: nav-badge on Match tab when you have an un-voted open ballot.
3
Single-pass ballot SHIPPED
Render the roster once, each player with a 🏆 and 🤡 button. Kills the DOTD drop-off directly.
4
Cut the candidate wall SHIPPED
Surface that night's goalscorers first with a ⚽ tag (and/or your team), "show everyone" expander, type-to-filter. Scanning 6 names beats scanning 36.
5
Pin it open SHIPPED
Default-expanded at the top of the Match tab while the window is live. Once closed, results return to the mid-slot.

What shipped (19 Jun 2026)

  • Single-pass ballot — one row per player, 🏆 + 🤡 buttons, both awards cast in one scroll.
  • Goalscorers sorted to the top with a ⚽ tag (from the goals table) so MOTM candidates surface without scrolling.
  • Search box to filter the roster live.
  • Pinned open while voting is live; collapses back to History-style mid-slot once closed.
  • Window close moved 09:00 → 10:00 the next day (open unchanged at 22:00 / full time).

Still open (backlog)

Reminders — web-push at full time ("🏆 Vote for tonight's MOTM") and on results publish ("Emmanuel won MOTM"); or, cheaper, a nav-badge nudge on the Match tab. This is the single biggest remaining lever.
  • Social proof / light shaming — "14 voted · 22 haven't" fits the fines-and-banter culture.
  • Voting streaks — give the regulars something to defend.
  • Results-reveal moment — make the award announcement feel like an event, not a quiet write to a row.
  • Context-mismatch bug — MotmVotingCard loads the latest voting window independently of the displayed match. During an open window before the result is entered, the page shows last week's result + report with this week's ballot wedged in. Worth tightening when this area is next touched.
