PREFERRED POSITION

Player-set position · the missing primitive
P703 · PROPOSAL
The idea — let each player pick a primary and secondary position from their profile. It's a 30-second action, unlocks position-aware stats and ratings, and gives the balancer the single most important constraint it's missing.

Why now

  • 10 of 36 active players have no position set — the balancer has nothing to work with. Admin-only field, no incentive for players to ask for it.
  • Stats are about to come alive (Wall, distance, goals/game) — interpreting them without position is misleading. A GK with 1 goal/season is a hero; a striker with 1 goal/season isn't.
  • The 18 Jun draft was lopsided on positions, not ratings — see below. v2 balancer can't fix this without position data.

How it works

Data model

Two new columns on profiles:

  • preferred_position_primary — required for new sign-ups, prompted at first login for existing players
  • preferred_position_secondary — optional, for the player who's "happy at MID, can fill in at DEF"

Four positions to keep it simple — football people have opinions about CDM vs CAM but a Sunday-night roster doesn't need it:

  • 🧤 GK — Goalkeeper
  • 🛡️ DEF — Defender
  • ⚙️ MID — Midfielder
  • ⚽ ATT — Forward

Player-set, not admin-set

Lives on the profile page as a single picker — primary big, secondary smaller. Players see it, players own it. Admin can still nudge ("you've been playing DEF all month, want to update?") via a one-tap prompt on the next-game screen.

What it unlocks

  • Position-aware overall — defender's "63 ATT" no longer drags their overall down. Each attribute weighted by relevance to the position they actually play.
  • Position-aware leaderboards on Stats — "Top 3 strikers by goals/game", "Top 3 defenders by Wall", "Top GK by clean sheets %". Specialists finally get celebrated.
  • Balancer constraint — every team gets ≥1 GK, ≥1 DEF, ≥1 ATT (whenever possible). Soft-balance to ~equal MID counts. The 18 Jun chaos becomes impossible.
  • Secondary position as the safety valve — if there's only one GK signed up but two players have GK as secondary, balancer pulls one across. No more "Chris Hughes is in goal by default."
  • Form rating context — peer 🟢 for a defender who kept a clean sheet means something different from peer 🟢 for a striker who scored. Weighted by position.

Sequencing into the existing roadmap

  1. Position picker on profile (this proposal) — week 1, low-risk, no rating changes.
  2. One-tap prompt on Next Game for the 10 players with no position set — week 1, drives adoption.
  3. Stats panels gated by position filter + 4th hero card for "Top GK" — week 2.
  4. Card redesign with prominent position badge + position rank pill — week 2.
  5. Balancer v2 with positional constraints — week 3, ships only once ≥80% of regulars have a position set.
  6. Engagement backlog (push, streaks, social proof) → then peer rating — separate workstream, runs in parallel.
