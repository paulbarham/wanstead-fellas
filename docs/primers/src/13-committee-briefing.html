What the app is for

Wanstead Fellas is a mobile web app that runs the whole Thursday-night football operation end-to-end — from Friday sign-up through match-day tactics to voting, fines and stats. It also carries the seasonal extras (World Cup predictor, sweepstake) that keep the group engaged between kick-offs.

Roadmap — what's queued next

Grouped by theme. Everything below is either agreed and in the queue (🟡), proposed but not yet scheduled (🟢), or has a dependency (⚠️) blocking release. Committee steer welcome on ordering.

⚽ Team tacticsFormationPicker just shipped
Next: Formation snapshot in match report (small pitch thumbnail per team in History) · position-played-per-player capture (feeds smarter stat weighting) · captain-gated formation edits (if free-for-all gets messy). In-app team chat is parked — WhatsApp already covers it.
🤝 Voting engagement44% turnout — ceiling to lift
Next: web push at full-time ("Vote for tonight's MOTM") · nav badge on Match tab when ballot open · voting streak counter · live social proof ("14 voted · 22 haven't") · Friday-morning results-reveal push. Peer-rating features (below) inherit voting turnout as a ceiling, so this ships first.
🤝 Peer ratings⚠️ Blocked on voting engagement
Design: a third inline column on the MOTM/DOTD ballot — rate each teammate 🟢 great · 🟡 OK · 🔴 off. Default 🟡 so no added friction. Feeds a small adjustment to the player's card rating (capped, Bayesian shrinkage for low sample size).
🛠️ Balancer v2Position gate cleared (83%)
Design: categorical balance (attribute spreads within ±X) · star cap (max 2 ≥ 85 OVR per team) · defender floor (≥1 with DEF ≥ 8) · simulated annealing · rematch penalty. Target: drop average score margin from ~2.5 → ~1.5 goals. Admin escape hatch (drag-swap + regenerate) stays.
🎭 Rating engine (live cards)Currently admin-set — going live-derived
Design: shooting from goals/appearance, defence from clean sheets, pace from fitness top-speed, physical from distance × HR-zone workload, passing from peer ratings. Nightly cron recomputes; deltas logged; form arrows (↗︎ / ↘︎) on cards; "Why this rating?" tooltip for transparency. Bayesian shrinkage for low-appearance players and MOTM/DOTD-influence capped to stop gaming.
💷 Money & enforcementCore done — automation left
Next: Stripe / online payment ("settle in one tap" from the app) · auto-apply credit when payment recorded (one entry, not two) · player-facing "you owe £X — pay by Sat" reminder · all-time finance view for the chase list.
📊 AdoptionThe fitness gap: 1/49 uploading
Design: "Most Distance Tonight" hero card on Stats · fitness line on player cards · Friday-morning nav badge on Cards for signed-up players · Strava connect-once auto-pull · "Complete your card" 30-second onboarding flow at first login · photo nudge on Cards tab (still 37 photo-less). The features are built — nobody's using them because there's no payoff loop or nudge.
📈 Data qualityNew signals for the rating engine
Next: position played per fixture (one-tap picker on match entry) · assists tracking (Veo recordings + shirt numbers being explored) · self-rated form Wednesday poll (🔥 💪 🆗 🤒 🛏️) · HR-zone fatigue index feeding the balancer.
🛂 Delegate rolesFirst role live (Ross Marks · scoring)
Next: Admin UI toggle to grant / revoke delegate roles per player · indicator on Admin profile page showing who holds each role · additional roles as needed (can_manage_fines, can_publish_teams).

Where committee steer would land best

Three real choices ahead.
  1. Sequencing. Engagement (voting push) vs Balancer v2 vs Rating engine — all sizeable. Which lands first?
  2. Ambition on cards. Live-derived ratings are transparent but noisy. Do we ship with training wheels (admin baseline ± small drift) or go straight to fully derived?
  3. Payments. Stripe integration is a bigger lift — do we want the frictionless "settle in one tap" or is cash-to-admin still fine?
Also worth knowing. Two features have adoption blockers we're aware of: Balancer v2 needs ≥80% of regulars to have a position set (currently 83% — cleared), and Peer ratings need voting turnout above ~65% (currently 44%) or the signal is too thin to trust. Both are addressed on the roadmap above.
