The fitness gap · why it isn't landing · what to do
P602 · ANALYSIS

The biggest gap: fitness uploads

Fitness uploaders
Polar accounts linked
Only the admin uploads.
3 sessions, 90 days. 2 via GPX file, 1 via Strava. The Polar integration that the team built lives behind a button nobody has tapped.

The data is there, the parsing works, the suggestions engine works. People just aren't bringing it to the app.

Why it isn't landing

🔍
1 · Discovery
Lives buried on the Cards page. Most opens are Sign-up, Match, Stats, Cup — nobody passes the upload button on a Thursday.
⛰️
2 · Effort
Demands a GPX/TCX export, a Polar link, or manual entry. Compared to a 2-tap MOTM vote, it's a chore.
🌑
3 · No payoff
Upload doesn't update your card stats. Doesn't appear on Stats leaderboards. No "Most Distance Tonight" hero. You upload into a void.
⏰
4 · No nudge
No prompt Friday morning, no nag if you've signed up. Voting at least has the pinned ballot — fitness has nothing.
👥
5 · No social proof
One uploader (the admin) means no peer pressure, no league table, no quiet shame in being the lazy one.
🔗
6 · Strava sync
Strava is where the data already lives for many. A "connect once, auto-pull Thursday's session" flow would beat any per-match upload prompt.

What to do — three levers ranked by impact ÷ effort

1
Surface fitness on Stats & Cards HIGH IMPACT · LOW EFFORT
Add a "Most Distance Tonight" hero on Stats and a k/m + avg HR line on each Card. Suddenly uploading earns you something visible — same loop that made the 100% sign-up rate work.
2
One-tap nudge after the match HIGH IMPACT · MED EFFORT
Friday-morning nav badge on Cards: "Upload your stats from last night" — same play that fixed voting. Pair with a copy tweak that makes manual entry (km + minutes) acceptable for non-wearable owners.
3
Strava connect-once, auto-pull HIGH IMPACT · HIGH EFFORT
OAuth + webhook so a Strava activity tagged Football auto-imports against that Thursday. Solves the effort problem permanently for the ~half the squad already on Strava.
Other quick wins worth noting.
· Secondary position prompt (4% set) — add a one-line nag inside the position picker after primary is saved.
· Profile photo prompt (35% set) — pin a soft nudge on the Cards tab for the 37 players without one; photos drive identity, which drives engagement.
· DOB / favourite club (32% / 28%) — could batch into a 30-second "complete your card" flow at first login.
