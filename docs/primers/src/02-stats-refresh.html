P502 · UPDATE
TL;DR — The Stats page just got a serious facelift. You no longer have to tap eight accordions to find anything. Headline awards sit at the top, every panel shows a top-3 podium under its title, and there's a brand-new defensive leaderboard. Open the app, tap Stats, see for yourself.

What's new at a glance

  • Hero strip at the top — three headline awards always visible.
  • Top-3 podium under every panel — gold / silver / bronze, no tapping required.
  • The Wall · NEW · a defensive leaderboard for goals conceded.
  • Sections — Attack · Awards · Defence · Participation · Shame.
  • My Stats pill — jumps to your player card.
  • One toggle at the top — flip the whole page between This Month and All Time.

The three headline awards now live front-and-centre at the top of Stats. Gold for top scorer, silver for the Wall King (lowest goals conceded), bronze for most wins. Updates with the page-level period filter.

🧱 NEW: The Wall — Goals Conceded

A defensive counterpart to the Winning Team stat. For every completed match, we sum up the goals your team shipped across all three fixtures on the night, then rank players by their average per night. Lower is better. Minimum 3 nights played to qualify (2 if you're viewing This Month).

Grouped into sections

The old flat list of nine accordions is now grouped under five teletext-style headings:

▮ Attack
▮ Awards
▮ Defence
▮ Participation
▮ Shame

Fines and DOTD live at the bottom under Shame. Awards (MOTM, Winning Team) live near the top where they belong.

👤 My Stats pill + global toggle

Above the hero strip you'll find a green My Stats pill (tap it to jump to your player card) and a single This Month / All Time toggle that filters every panel and hero card at once.

What hasn't changed

  • The full ranked tables are still there — they're just one tap behind the chevron now.
  • The Fines filter chips (All / Late / Drop Out / etc.) still work the same way inside that panel.
  • Voting, sign-ups, the World Cup hub, fixtures — all untouched. Stats only.
