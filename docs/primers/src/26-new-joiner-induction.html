Welcome in. You've joined Wanstead Fellas — a Thursday-night 8-a-side football group in E11 that's been running since 2020 and grown to ~70 fellas across every age from Under-20s to 50+. What follows is everything you need to know in three pages. Read once, come Thursday.

A Wanstead Fellas Thursday

📍 Where
Eton Manor, Wanstead E11. On the main astroturf.
🕘 When
Every Thu · 21:00–22:00 KO · arrive 20:45.
⚽ Format
Either 11-a-side or a 4-team round-robin. Subject to numbers.
🎒 Bring
Boots · shirt (any colour) · water. Bibs supplied.

What a typical night looks like

🕘 20:45
Arrive. Teams are already published — check the Teams tab in the app on the way over so you know who you're with.
⚽ 21:00 → 22:00
Play. Round-robin nights: three ~20-min games, every team plays every other team, rolling subs. 11-a-side nights: one hour-long match. Bibs vs shirts either way.
🍺 22:15+
Drinks in the clubhouse. Optional but recommended — where the group gets to know each other, and most of the banter for next week's match report gets minted.
🗳️ Fri 10am
Awards ballot closes. Vote MOTM, DOTD, and (when set) Theme of the Night — winners auto-announced, match report drops with the results.
👋 Introduce yourself. If it's your first night, find Paul Barham, Tom Broughton, Edd Ezra, Scott Duncan, Lawrie Pointer — or honestly any other fella at pitch-side — and say hello. They'll point out who's who and make sure you know the drill. Everyone else will make themselves known — nobody stays a stranger past the first game.

Selection & reserves

Sign up on Tonight by Wed 10pm. Priority when the game fills (32-cap): SUB → WTP★ → WTP. WTPs get pushed to the waiting list once full; SUBs and WTP★ can bump WTPs. Reserves auto-promote the moment someone drops out (WTP★ first). Drop out after Wed 10pm without a replacement → fine.

The app — 60-second orientation

The whole club runs off wanstead-fellas.vercel.app. It's a free web app — no App Store, no download. Open the URL, hit "Add to Home Screen" (iPhone Safari) or "Install" (Android Chrome) and you're set. It'll behave like a proper app after that.

FIRST FIVE MINUTES
1
Sign up with your email + password.
2
Fill your Profile — photo, name, age band, preferred position (GK / DEF / MID / ATT), preferred foot, favourite club. Two minutes.
3
Enable notifications — the one-tap toggle on Profile. How you'll hear about teams being published, match reports going live, and dropouts.
4
Sign up for Thursday — Tonight tab → "Mark Me In". Deadline is Wednesday 10pm.
5
Have a poke around the other tabs — Cards, History, Predictor. All will make more sense after your first Thursday.

Costs and player tiers

Three tiers, priority in this order when the game oversubscribes (max 32/night):

SUB
£95 / season
Full season paid up front. Guaranteed spot every week. Most regular players are on this.
WTP★
£5 / game
Long-standing "Wait To Play" players with priority — bumped up ahead of standard WTP when spots are tight.
WTP
£5 / game
Pay-as-you-play. Fill remaining spots on a first-come basis. Most new joiners start here.

Fees are tracked in-app under Profile → My Finances. Pay by bank transfer (details are on the Finances page). Grace period is 2 weeks past a match — beyond that, unpaid balances block future sign-ups until settled.

For the avoidance of doubt: this isn't a money-making scheme — it's to keep kick-off on time, cut the ball-replacement bill, and remind everyone it's a kickabout (and a beer) with likeminded people from the local area.

And the rest

🏆 Awards
MOTM (best on the pitch) and DOTD (spectacular own-goals, air-kicks, meltdowns). Occasional themed award. Vote closes 10am Fri.
🩹 Injury list
Picked up a knock? Profile → Mark myself injured. Group sees you're out; app warns if you sign up while marked injured (doesn't block).
💬 WhatsApp vs app
WhatsApp for banter and last-minute shouts. App for anything with a deadline (sign-ups, votes, fines, predictor).
Any questions: Feedback tab in the app goes straight to Paul. Category "General" if you're not sure. Never goes unread.
