Welcome in. You've joined Wanstead Fellas — a Thursday-night 8-a-side football group in E11 that's been running since 2020 and grown to ~70 fellas across every age from Under-20s to 50+. What follows is everything you need to know in three pages. Read once, come Thursday.

A Wanstead Fellas Thursday

Where: Eton Manor, Wanstead E11. Astroturf pitches down the far end.
When: Every Thursday, 21:00–22:00 kick-off — arrive 20:45 at the latest.
Format: Usually a four-team 8v8 round-robin — six 20-minute games. Occasionally a straight 10v10 or a tournament. Always social, always competitive.
Bring: Boots or astro trainers. Shirt (any colour — one team plays in bibs, the other in shirts). Water.

What a typical night looks like

  1. Teams are already published before you arrive — check the Teams tab in the app on the way over.
  2. You'll play three short games — every team plays every other team, ~20 mins each. Rolling subs.
  3. Awards are voted the next morning in-app — Man of the Match, Dick of the Day, sometimes a themed award (e.g. "Peak dad-strength").
  4. After the game the drinks are in the clubhouse — optional but recommended, and where most of the banter for next week's report gets minted.
👋 Introduce yourself. If it's your first night, find Paul Barham, Tom Broughton, Edd Ezra, Scott Duncan, Lawrie Pointer — or honestly any other fella at pitch-side — and say hello. They'll point out who's who and make sure you know the drill. Everyone else will make themselves known — nobody stays a stranger past the first game.

Under-the-radar rules

Four unwritten ones that the app can't enforce:

  • Play through niggles when you can — but log real injuries in the app so admin can plan (see p3).
  • Don't be a cunt. There's literally a fine for this. It's called Cuntiness.
  • Buy your round. On pitch and off it.
  • Bibs go home, get washed, come back. If you played in bibs, take yours home and wash it — don't chuck it damp into the kit bag. Nobody wants to pull on last week's.

The app — 60-second orientation

The whole club runs off wanstead-fellas.vercel.app. It's a free web app — no App Store, no download. Open the URL, hit "Add to Home Screen" (iPhone Safari) or "Install" (Android Chrome) and you're set. It'll behave like a proper app after that.

FIRST FIVE MINUTES

  1. Sign up with your email + password.
  2. Go to Profile — add a photo, name, age band, preferred position (GK / DEF / MID / ATT), preferred foot, favourite club. Two minutes.
  3. Enable notifications — the one-tap toggle on Profile. This is how you'll hear about teams being published, match reports going live, and dropouts.
  4. Go to Tonight and hit "Mark Me In" for the next game. Do this before Wednesday 10pm.
  5. Have a poke around the other tabs — Cards, History, Predictor. All will make more sense after your first Thursday.

The two things that trip new joiners up:  (1) Notifications not enabled — you'll miss the "teams are ready" ping and the match report drop. Fix it once on Profile.  (2) Not adding a photo — cards look drab, and the group can't put a face to your name. Takes 5 seconds.

Awards, injury list & the rest

🏆 Awards. After every match: Man of the Match (best on the pitch) and Dick of the Day (least — usually a spectacular own-goal, air-kick or full-blown meltdown). Occasional themed award set by admin (Kevin Keegan tribute, Italian football, etc.). Voting closes 10am Friday; winners auto-announced.

🩹 Injury list. Picked up a knock? Profile → Mark myself injured → pick a type + when you're back. The group sees a "🩹 On the injury list" strip on the Tonight tab so nobody chases you for numbers. Play through niggles if you can — the app warns if you sign up while marked injured, but doesn't block.

💬 WhatsApp vs the app. WhatsApp for banter and last-minute shouts. App for anything with a deadline (sign-ups, votes, fines, predictor). Admin lives in the app; the group chat is the pub.

Any questions: Feedback tab in the app goes straight to Paul. Category "General" if you're not sure. Zero-friction, no reply expected within any particular window — but it never goes unread.
