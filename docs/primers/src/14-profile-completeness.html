How we're closing the gap

🟢 Just shipped · ProfileCompletionCard

Live on Next Game · 4 Jul 2026

Everyone who signs into Next Game with at least one profile gap sees a single green "Complete your card" banner at the top of the page. It shows a progress bar (N of 6 done) and one tappable row per missing field. Position, backup position, foot and age band can be picked inline; photo and favourite club deep-link to the Profile page. The banner auto-hides at 6 of 6 and is dismissable per session.

This replaces the two standalone position + foot nudges that were doing the same job less coherently. One banner instead of three.

What each row does

  • Position · inline 4-button picker (GK / DEF / MID / ATT)
  • Backup position · same picker, second tap = secondary
  • Foot · inline 3-button picker (LEFT / RIGHT / BOTH)
  • Age · inline 5-band picker (U20 / 20s / 30s / 40s / 50+), with a link to Profile for anyone happy to share exact DOB
  • Photo · deep-link to Profile page (photo upload flow)
  • Favourite club · deep-link to Profile page (long club dropdown)

All saves are instant. Progress bar updates live. Dismiss ✕ hides it for this session only — it comes back next login until 6 of 6 done.

Expected impact on the numbers above

  • Secondary position (5% → target 40%+): now the fifth of only six rows anyone with a gap sees on their tab. Same treatment as primary, but only prompts once primary is set.
  • Foot (5% → target 60%+): single tap for the vast majority of the squad who know their foot. Progress bar completion motivation.
  • Photo (34% → target 60%+): "PROFILE →" link is one tap, upload takes 20 seconds. Committee steer welcome on whether we surface a specific "photo of the week" nudge on the Cards tab.
  • Favourite club (29% → target 50%+): should track photo closely — one is not much harder than the other once someone's on the Profile page.
  • Age (88% → target 95%+): already high because of the band fallback. The 7 red ✗ players are almost all inactive-ish WTPs with minimal profile — chasing them by name (list on p1-2) is likely more effective than another nudge.

A follow-up pull in 2 weeks will show how much the banner has moved the needle. This audit was baseline; the next one will be delta.
