TL;DR — Once the teams are published on the Match tab, every team now shows a pitch with slots for goalkeeper, defence, midfield, attack. Pick your shape (2-3-1, 3-2-1, 2-2-2, …), drop yourself and your teammates into positions, and settle the tactics before kick-off. Anyone on the roster can edit — start the argument early.

Where it lives

Match tab. It appears the moment teams are published for the next Thursday and disappears when the result is entered. Two cards, one per team, stacked vertically.

Everyone's on the pitch.

Reading the pitch top-to-bottom = attack → midfield → defence → GK. Same orientation as FIFA: your keeper's at the base, you're attacking upward.

How to pick a shape

The chips row across the top lists every formation that fits your roster size. Tap one and the pitch reshuffles. Auto-fill puts each player in their preferred position (see below).

2-3-1DEFAULT
3-2-1SOLID
2-2-2BALANCED
3-1-2COUNTER
1-3-2GUNG-HO
1-4-1MIDFIELD

Numbers read DEF – MID – ATT; goalkeeper is implicit. 5v5 (2-1-1, 1-2-1, 1-1-2) and 8v8 (3-3-1, 2-3-2, 2-4-1, …) shapes also available when the roster size matches.

How to assign a player

1

Tap a slot

2

Tap a bench player

The bench pills below the pitch turn green while a slot is selected. Tap one:

MWM. WinterMID SDS. DuncanATT

Player drops into the slot; the pill disappears from the bench.

3

Swap or clear

Tap an occupied slot to swap — pick a bench player and they take over (the previous occupant drops back to the bench). Or hit CLEAR to empty the slot.

Auto-fill

The ⚡ AUTO-FILL button top-right re-runs the suggester:

  1. GK first — anyone whose primary position is GK; otherwise the strongest secondary GK; otherwise whoever's left.
  2. Then DEF / MID / ATT rows — matched by primary position first, then secondary, then whoever's left.

The more players have set a primary + secondary position on their Profile page, the better this gets. Currently 83% of the squad have set a primary; 4% have set a secondary (see the app engagement review — chase that number up and auto-fill practically does the whole job for you).

Who can edit

▶ CAN EDIT

  • Anyone on the team's roster
  • Admin
  • Parents acting on behalf of a linked child on the roster

■ READ-ONLY

  • Players on the other team (they can look, but the shape/AUTO-FILL/slots are frozen)
  • Reserves who didn't get subbed in
Consequence: If seven of you all live-edit at the same time on Thursday evening, last-write-wins. Don't overthink it — the shape is a talking point, not a legal contract. Talk it out in the group chat first if you're all about to fiddle.

What it saves

  • Shape — one of 20-odd formation codes.
  • Slots — a map of slot → player, e.g. gk: Peter May, def_0: Daren Low, …
  • Who last edited it + when — for the audit trail if there's a dispute.

Save is auto — half a second after your last tap. Watch the header pill: SAVING… → ✓ SAVED. No submit button.

What hasn't changed

  • Sign-up, sign-out, reserve promotion, WTP charges — all untouched.
  • The team lineup itself (who's on which team) is still set in the Team Builder by admin.
  • Match results, MOTM/DOTD voting, match reports — same as before.
