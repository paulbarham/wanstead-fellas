WANSTEAD FELLAS

Match Night Notifications · Player Setup
P518 · GUIDE
Turn on match-night notifications and you'll get a quiet ping on your phone when it's time to vote for MOTM & DOTD, and again when the results are in. Takes 30 seconds. One-time setup per device. No downloads, no accounts, no ads. Do it once and enjoy Thursdays a bit more.

What you'll get

🏆 After tonight's final whistle
"Vote for tonight's awards"
Fired as soon as the admin submits the result. Tap it and you land straight on the ballot — a couple of taps and you're voted for MOTM and DOTD before you've even reached the car park.
📊 Friday morning-ish
"MOTM & DOTD results"
When the results are published, another ping tells you who won. Chase your streak — every week you vote adds one to your 🔥 counter on the ballot.

Pick your phone

🍎 iPHONE / iPAD

Setup — 30 seconds

1 Add the app to your Home Screen — this is a one-time thing that iPhones require for notifications to work. In Safari, open wanstead-fellas.vercel.app. Tap the 📤 Share button at the bottom. Scroll and pick "Add to Home Screen". Tap Add in the top-right.
2 Open the app from the Home Screen icon — the black-and-yellow WF icon that just appeared on your phone. Not from Safari. This bit really matters — Safari-launched app can't do notifications on iPhone.
3 Tap your avatar (top-right of any screen). Scroll down to the 🔔 Match-night notifications card.
4 Tap "Enable notifications". When your phone asks "Allow notifications?", tap Allow. The card flips to green ON. That's it — you're done.
iOS 16.4 or newer needed (any iPhone from the last few years). If your iPhone is really old, updating iOS is the way in.
🤖 ANDROID

Setup — 15 seconds

1 Open the app in Chrome — wanstead-fellas.vercel.app. No Home Screen faff needed on Android, notifications work straight from the browser.
2 Tap your avatar (top-right of any screen). Scroll down to the 🔔 Match-night notifications card.
3 Tap "Enable notifications". When Chrome asks "Allow notifications?", tap Allow. The card flips to green ON. Done.
Bonus: if you want the app icon on your Home Screen too (nicer than opening Chrome each week), Chrome menu → "Add to Home Screen". Optional — notifications work either way on Android.

Something not working?

Q: I tapped Enable but nothing happens. Most common cause on iPhone: you're on Safari, not the Home Screen app. Go back to step 1 — add to Home Screen first, then reopen the app from that icon.

Q: I tapped Block by mistake and now I can't turn them on. iPhone: Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data → wanstead-fellas.vercel.app, then re-enable. Android: Chrome menu → Settings → Site settings → Notifications → find the site, allow it. Easier: uninstall the Home Screen icon and start again from step 1.

Q: Turn them off / nothing arrived on Thursday. Same card → "Turn off on this device" to disable, same button to re-enable. Missed a Thursday? Check Do Not Disturb / Focus modes aren't muting the app.
