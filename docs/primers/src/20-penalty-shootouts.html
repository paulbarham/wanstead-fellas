Penalty Shootouts · Update
P520 · UPDATE
TL;DR — In the spirit of the World Cup, drawn games now go to a penalty shootout. The shootout winner takes a +1 bonus point in the table. Nothing else about a normal win or loss changes. Shootout pens are not goals — they don't touch the top-scorer race, though they might sway the MOTM / DOTD chat.

The new scoring, at a glance

Win in normal time
3
unchanged
Draw
1
each team, as before
Win the shootout
+1
bonus, on top of the draw point

So a shootout winner finishes on 2 and the loser on 1 — a shootout win is worth more than a draw, less than a proper win. A regulation win is still the full 3.

Worked example. Reds 2–2 Blues, Blues win the shootout → Blues +2 pts (1 draw + 1 bonus), Reds +1 pt. If Reds had won 3–2 in normal time it'd be the usual 3–0 pts split — no shootout.

Where it happens

Entirely inside Admin → Match Entry, the same screen you already use to key in scores and scorers. There's nothing new to learn for a decisive game — the shootout controls only appear when a fixture finishes level. Round-robin nights: every drawn fixture gets its own shootout.

Key the level score

Tap the score steppers as normal until both sides read the same — e.g. 2 – 2.

Pick the shootout winner

A yellow Penalties bar appears under the score. Tap the team that won on pens — it locks in yellow. Tapped the wrong one? Tap the other, or tap again to clear.

3

Submit as usual

That's it — the bonus point lands in the table automatically. Submit stays locked until every drawn fixture has a winner picked, so you can't forget one.

The 0–0 special case

A goalless game still goes to pens — but the score steppers can't show a real 0 – 0 (walking both to zero just means "not played yet"). So for a goalless draw you'll see an explicit button first:

Reds FC
− 0 + v − 0 +
Blues FC
· mark 0-0 draw (→ penalties) ·

Tap mark 0-0 draw and the fixture records as a genuine goalless draw, then the same Penalties — pick winner bar appears. Pick the winner and submit.

What it does to the table

The bonus flows straight into the live table, the published result card, and the Stats "champion team" — all three in step. In our example, the shootout nudges Blues one point clear of the side they drew with:

Result cards spell it out too: ▶ DRAW · BLUES FC WIN ON PENS, and the copy-to-WhatsApp report adds "(Blues FC won on pens)" next to the score.

What counts — and what doesn't

■ Pens are NOT goals

  • No shootout scorers are recorded.
  • The top-scorer race is completely untouched.
  • Don't enter shootout pens in the scorers list.

▶ Pens CAN colour the night

  • Voters can weigh a shootout hero (or villain) in MOTM / DOTD — that stays free-form, your call.
  • The result card shows who won, so voters see it.

What hasn't changed

  • Decisive games — enter exactly as before. No shootout controls appear.
  • A regulation win is still 3 points; a loss is still 0.
  • Scorers, own goals, match report, highlights, MOTM/DOTD voting — all identical.
