Duos & rivals
3 spots

Head-to-head stats for every pair of players who've shared a pitch. Backed by every fixture in the app's history, refreshed nightly. Same materialised data powers three different surfaces so it's noticed even if you don't visit Profile.

📊 Stats tab
Duo of the Month hero card — highest win-rate pair with 3+ games together this month, sitting alongside Top Scorer / Wall King / Most Wins / Top GK. August's winner: Callum Finch + Sheridan Winter, 100% across 4 games.
👥 Tonight tab
Best duo tonight — one line at the bottom of each published team card. "🤝 Best duo · Ross + Daren · 3W-1D". Pre-match banter without anyone doing anything.
👤 Profile
Your top duos + biggest rivals — top 3 of each, season-scoped default with an All-Time toggle. Skips pairs with fewer than 5 fixtures so it doesn't render statistical noise.

Monthly personal review
Profile · every 1st

Per-player wrap of the month gone. Positive-only — highlights, streaks, favourite teammate. Not a leaderboard, doesn't rank you against anyone else.

Notification prompts (adoption push)
Tonight + Profile

Only 18 of 86 profiles have notifications enabled — meaning most of the group misses call-ups off the waiting list, voting-open pings, match-report releases, monthly wraps and Duo of the Month callouts. Two low-risk nudges to lift that:

✅ Completion card
The "👋 Complete your card" panel on the Tonight tab is now 7/7 fields, not 6/6. New "🔔 Notifications" row with a one-tap Enable button. Ticks green as soon as any of your devices is subscribed — no double-nagging if you enabled on desktop and now open on phone.
🔔 Tonight banner
The existing top-of-tab prompt has been rewritten louder, with a punchier value prop ("You'll miss call-ups off the waiting list · voting opens · match reports · monthly wraps · duo callouts"). Dismissable for 7 days at a time.

How to check yours: on Profile the existing "Push notifications" card shows whether you're on and lets you toggle it. Once on, the five category chips let you mute individual streams (match night · results · money · club news · predictor) — absence = everything on, so nothing needed unless you want less.
