What v2 would have done

The new algorithm balances on every category, not just overall. Constraints layered on top:

  • Categorical balance: each attribute total within ±4 across teams.
  • GK guarantee: each team gets ≥1 recognised GK.
  • Defender floor: each team gets ≥1 player with DEF ≥ 8.
  • Star cap: max 2 players with OVR ≥ 8 per team.
  • Simulated annealing: random swap pairs of equal-OVR players to drive imbalance down. ~1000 iterations, <100ms.

Why it matters over the season

  • Closer games — fewer 6-0 stomps means more fun for everyone.
  • Fairer "Winning Team" stat — wins will reflect player quality more than draft luck.
  • Trustable "Wall" stat — GA per night becomes a real defensive signal once defences aren't lopsided.
  • Validation: after 4 weeks of v2, expect avg score differential to drop from ~2.5 → ~1.5 goals.
Admin escape hatch — admin can still drag-swap players post-balance. The algorithm is a suggestion, not a mandate. Add a "regenerate" button to try a fresh allocation if a particular output looks off.

RATE YOUR SQUAD

Peer rating · low-friction design
P702 · PROPOSAL
The problem — MOTM/DOTD engagement is low because it feels altruistic and takes too many clicks. Peer ratings could be gold for the balancer, but only if 60%+ of the squad actually does them every week. This proposal designs around the engagement gap, not the data we want.

Design principles

  • ≤ 20 seconds total — finished before you've cracked your second beer.
  • One screen, no scrolling — the whole squad visible at once.
  • One tap per teammate — three colours, no text input, no comments.
  • Selfish incentive — gate your rating behind doing the work.
  • Combined with MOTM/DOTD — same screen, not three separate flows.

The behaviour loop

📲 Trigger

Push notification fires 10pm Thursday (after the match): "How was the squad? 20 seconds and your form rating updates." Single tap deep-links straight to this screen. No menu navigation required.

🔒 Selfish hook

Player's own form rating (a single 1–5 number on their card) is hidden behind a lock icon until they've rated their own teammates. Same psychology as "see who liked you" on Hinge — give to get.

🔥 Streak

Top of the screen: "🔥 4 weeks in a row · keep the streak". Breaking a streak feels worse than starting one. Players will tap through just to keep the flame alive.

👻 Anonymity

Players see their aggregated form (e.g. "Your squad rated you 4.2"), but never who rated what. Removes social cost and frees honest ratings.

🏆 MOTM & DOTD baked in

After the 7 colour taps, the final screen shows the players you tagged 🟢 and asks "Pick MOTM". One tap. Same for 🔴 → DOTD. Total: ~9 taps. No more separate flow.

🔇 Forgetters

If you forget, the screen still works any time before Friday 9am. After: locked, your form rating doesn't update for that week (small penalty — incentive without punishment).

What the data unlocks

  • Form rating — fold the rolling 4-match peer score into the card's overall with ±3 weight. Cards feel "alive".
  • Balancer signal — a player on a hot peer-rated streak gets a small synthetic boost; one on a cold streak gets cooled. Stops "the lazy week" from being silently rewarded by the balancer.
  • MOTM & DOTD higher quality — comes from people who actually watched the match (the squad), not casual viewers.
  • Engagement metric — track "% of squad who rated" each week. Initial target: 60%. Aim 75% by month 3.
