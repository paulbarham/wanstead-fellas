What the app is for

The app is built on five pillars. Every feature belongs to one of these — that's how to make sense of the menu.

🛠️
Operations
Sign up, build fair teams, capture scores. The boring backbone — done well so nobody thinks about it.
🎭
Stats Theatre
Leaderboards, podiums, MOTM/DOTD, banter-fuel. Make every Thursday feel like a season finale.
🪪
Identity
Your Top Trumps card · position · badges · cuntiness · club. Your in-app persona.
⚖️
Fairness
Balanced teams, tier-based bumping, auto WTP fees, transparent rules. Removes admin from the firing line.
🤝
Community
World Cup hub, sweepstake, pods, history. Glue that keeps the group together between matches.

Quick start (5 minutes)

1 Open the app link your captain sent you. Tap Sign up → email + password. Confirm your name. You're in.

2 Install on your home screen so it behaves like a proper app: iPhone Safari → share → Add to Home Screen; Android Chrome → menu → Install app.

3 Pick your position — first time you visit Next Game you'll see a one-tap prompt for 🧤 GK · 🛡️ DEF · ⚙️ MID · ⚽ ATT. Picks a primary and (optionally) a backup. Helps the auto-balancer build fairer teams.

Fairness rule: who bumps who

When the game's full, the priority order kicks in:

  • Subscribed can bump WTP+ and WTP (newest-confirmed first).
  • WTP+ can bump WTP only.
  • WTP joins the waiting list.

If anyone drops out, the top reserve gets auto-promoted instantly. No admin action needed.

Common how-tos

🆘 The match is full

You join the reserves (yellow status). The list is auto-ranked by tier + signup time. If someone drops, the top reserve gets bumped in.

👶 Sign up your kid

If admin has linked your child to your account, you'll see them as a small pill next to your I'm In button. Tap to sign them up; tap again to drop.

🚪 Drop out

Tap your green I'm In button → confirm. If anyone's waiting, your slot goes to the top reserve automatically. You may not get your spot back.

🔒 Miss the 10pm Wednesday deadline?

Sign-ups lock. The admin can still slot you in if there's room — ping in WhatsApp.

💷 Why was I charged £5?

If you're a WTP or WTP+ player, signing up confirmed automatically logs a £5 fee for the night. Drop out before kick-off and the (unpaid) fee is reaped. See page 4.

Your status is always written on the big button:
✓ I'm In · 🟡 On the Waiting List · ⏳ Reserve (was confirmed, got bumped)

👥 Teams (Wednesday evening onwards)

Once admin publishes the teams (Wed 10pm onwards), the Teams tab shows your line-up, team name, team colours and captain. Captain is auto-picked from the top three rated players via a random draw — so the same person doesn't always wear it.

Auto-sizing picks the format based on confirmed players (32 → 4×8, 22 → 2×11, 18 → 2×9, 10 → 2×5, etc.).

📝 Friday: the match report

The report drops on the Match tab Friday morning. Block order:

  1. Group table — final standings for the night
  2. Predicted vs Actual — table snapshot vs the night-before forecast
  3. Results — each fixture's score with scorers
  4. Match Awards — MOTM + DOTD with vote counts
  5. Match Report — summary, key highlights, banter, app watch

Older reports live on History (under More) — same layout when you expand a row.

📈 Match Fitness

On your Profile, tap + Add match fitness. Either:

  • Upload a .gpx or .tcx file from your watch app (Polar, Garmin, Strava). The app reads distance, duration, max speed, avg HR, HR zones automatically.
  • Type the numbers in by hand if you didn't track.

Re-uploading for the same date replaces the previous entry. Distance feeds the leaderboard; the rest will power smarter card attributes (see page 6).

📅 History

Every past Thursday in chronological order. Tap a row to expand — same layout as the Match tab (table → predicted vs actual → results → awards → report). Great for re-living the worldie you scored 4 weeks ago.

🃏 Cards (the gallery)

Every player's Top Trumps card in one place. Browse, compare, screenshot for banter. Admin can edit ratings here too.

🎧 Pods

Curated football podcasts + news feed. Your favourite club (set on Profile) gets its own section at the top — your club's official pods plus the wider Wanstead Fellas list.

✉️ Feedback

Bug? Feature request? Design gripe? Send it through the Feedback page. Categories:

  • Bug Report — broken thing, which screen.
  • Feature Request — what you'd love to see.
  • Design Feedback — looks off / could look better.
  • General — anything else.

Admin reviews them weekly. Most of v3 of the app came from feedback like this.

🏆 World Cup (during tournaments)

Appears as the 5th bottom tab while a tournament's running. Four sub-tabs:

My PicksYour scorecard — what you picked, what's still locked

Picks lock 5 min before kick-off. Miss it and that match counts as a wrong pick — the league denominator is all settled matches, not just the ones you predicted. Set a reminder for early-morning games.

💷 My Finances

Open via More → My Finances. Three sections:

  • Outstanding balance hero — big number, red if owed, green if clear.
  • WTP Games This Month — for casual players, a £5 fee auto-logs when you sign up confirmed. Shows count + £ total + per-game list.
  • Outstanding Fines — late (£2), drop-out (£2), lost ball (£3), cuntiness (£5).
  • Paid items — collapsible history, opens on tap.
You owe nothing automatically. Even WTP fees show as unpaid until admin marks them paid. Tap "All clear ✓" to confirm.

⚙️ Admin (admin-only)

Hidden for everyone else. Four tabs:

  • Players — edit any player's stats, card, GK attrs, position, club, age, type, badges, photos. Position Adoption Tracker at the top shows who still hasn't picked a position (chase list).
  • Finance — fines + WTP fees, mark paid, delete, CSV export by month.
  • Families — link parent ↔ child profiles.
  • Feedback — review submissions.

Behind the scenes (things you'll never notice)

  • 32-cap enforced server-side — even if two players hit Sign Up at exactly the same moment, one goes to reserves.
  • WTP £5 auto-charged the instant you flip to confirmed; auto-reaped (unpaid only) if you drop out before kick-off.
  • World Cup scores pulled every 30 minutes from football-data.org. Admin-entered results are never overwritten.
  • Realtime sign-ups — the playing list updates live without you needing to refresh.
  • PWA offline cache — the app still opens with no signal; just won't show fresh data.
  • Position Adoption Tracker (admin) — chase list for anyone who hasn't set a position.

🛟 When something looks wrong

  1. Force-refresh. iPhone: swipe up, re-open. Desktop: Cmd+Shift+R. Fixes 90% of "weird" data.
  2. Check signal — most live features need it.
  3. Still wrong? Drop the admin a message: pabarham@gmail.com or in the WhatsApp group, with a screenshot.
