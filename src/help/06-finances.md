# Finances — WTP fees, fines, credits

The **My Finances** section on your Profile shows what you owe and what you've paid.

## WTP fees — the £5 pay-per-play

If you're on the **Want-To-Play** or **WTP Priority** tier, you're charged **£5 for each match you actually play**. The charge lands automatically the morning after — so if you sign up but end up on the reserve list, or drop out, **you're not charged**.

Subscribed players pay monthly instead — no per-match charge appears on your Finances tab.

## Fines

The four fine types:

| Fine | Amount | When it lands |
|---|---|---|
| **Late** | £2 | You arrived after kick-off |
| **Lost Ball** | £3 | You lost a ball during the match |
| **Cuntiness** | £5 | Admin's discretion — for consistent moaning, arguing with the ref, etc. |
| **Drop-out** | £2 | You pulled out after Wed 10pm without a reserve stepping in |

Fines are issued by admin from the Admin panel. You'll see the entry in your Finances tab with a note explaining what it's for.

## Credits

If you overpay, or admin owes you money for whatever reason (goodwill, refund, etc.), you'll see a **credit** — a negative balance. Credits auto-apply to your next charge; e.g. if you owe £5 for a WTP week and have a £3 credit sitting there, you'll only see £2 outstanding.

## The 2-week grace period + block

If you owe money, you get **two weeks** to settle up. After that, your account is **blocked from signing up** for future games. You'll see a red banner on the Next Game tab saying "You owe £X — pay to unlock".

Only the admin can override the block. Get in touch and settle up.

## Monthly invoice

At the end of each month, admin sends a summary of what everyone owes to the WhatsApp group. Pay by whichever method the group uses (bank transfer, cash to admin, etc.) and admin marks you paid in the app.
