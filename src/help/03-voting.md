# Voting — MOTM & DOTD

After every match you get to vote:

- 🏆 **MOTM** — Man of the Match (best player)
- 🤡 **DOTD** — Dick of the Day (worst moment, biggest blunder, funniest own-goal)

## When it opens

The voting window opens **as soon as the admin submits the full-time result** — typically Thursday around 22:00 — and closes at **Friday 10:00 am**. About 12 hours.

If you've enabled notifications, you'll get a **push** the moment it opens. Otherwise there's a **red dot on the Match tab** icon in the nav until you've cast both votes.

## How the ballot works

Open the 📊 **Match** tab. You'll see one row per rostered player, in this order:

- **Tonight's goalscorers first** (with a ⚽ tag) — these are your natural MOTM candidates
- Then everyone else, sorted alphabetically
- A search box up top lets you jump to a name

Tap 🏆 next to your MOTM pick, then 🤡 next to your DOTD pick. Both votes save instantly. You can change your mind until the window closes.

## Streaks 🔥

Vote for **two matches in a row** and a streak counter appears on the ballot: `🔥 2-week streak · vote to keep it alive`. It goes up by 1 every time you vote in a match you were rostered for. Missing a match you weren't rostered for **doesn't** break your streak — signup capacity misses don't punish you.

## Results

Once the window closes, the results are tallied automatically and published. The winner is shown at the top of the Match tab; ties are marked "shared".

Admin can override the result if the vote is stitched (e.g. someone was clearly MOTM but not on the roster) — this shows up as an **ADMIN CALL** badge on the result.
