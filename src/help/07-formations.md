# Team formations & tactics

Once teams are published on Wednesday night, each side gets a **pitch** on the Match tab where you can pick a shape and drop teammates into positions. Everyone on the team can edit — think of it as a whiteboard for pre-match debate rather than a formal thing.

## Where to find it

📊 **Match** tab, before Thursday's kick-off. Two cards, one per team, stacked vertically. Each shows:

- **Shape selector** at the top — 2-3-1, 3-2-1, 2-2-2, 3-1-2, 1-3-2, 1-4-1 (or the equivalents for 5v5 / 6v6 / 8v8 depending on team size)
- **Pitch** below — GK at the bottom, then DEF row, MID row, ATT row at the top (FIFA orientation, attacking upward)
- **Bench** at the very bottom — the players who aren't on the pitch yet
- **AUTO-FILL** button top-right — re-runs the position-aware suggester

## How to move players

1. **Tap a slot** on the pitch — it highlights yellow
2. **Tap a player** in the bench — they get dropped into that slot
3. **Tap an occupied slot** to swap or clear it

Or hit **AUTO-FILL** and the app assigns players based on their preferred position from their Profile.

## Who can edit

- ✅ **Anyone on that team's roster** can edit
- ✅ **Admin** can edit any team
- ❌ **Players on the other team** see it read-only

## Saving

Auto-saves as you tap. Header shows `SAVING…` → `✓ SAVED` so you know it landed.

## What if we disagree?

Last-write-wins. If seven of you live-edit at the same time on Thursday evening, someone's shape will get overwritten. Not a big deal — the formation is a talking point, not a legal contract. Discuss in WhatsApp first if you're all about to fiddle.

## What it doesn't do

Doesn't affect the actual line-up on the pitch — this is a suggestion / talking point. On the night the captains still call it, subs still happen, and no-one's checking against the app during play.
