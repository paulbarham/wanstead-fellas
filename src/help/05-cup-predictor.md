# World Cup Predictor — how scoring works

During a tournament, the 🏆 **World Cup** tab appears in the bottom nav. There's a **Predictor** and a **Sweepstake** — this article covers the predictor scoring rules.

## Group stage — 1 point per correct pick

Each group match is a **1X2** pick:

- **1** = home team wins
- **X** = draw
- **2** = away team wins

Get it right → **1 point**. Get it wrong → 0. That simple.

Picks **lock 5 minutes before kick-off** — after that the row goes read-only.

## Knockouts — 2 points on offer

Knockouts split the score into two parts:

| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| **Correct team advances** | 1 point |
| **Correct method** (90 mins / extra time / penalties) | +1 bonus point |
| **Total possible per match** | 2 points |

So if you pick "France beats Paraguay in 90 mins" and it goes to penalties, you get 1 point (right team) but miss the bonus. If you pick "Norway beats Brazil in ET" and it happens in 90, same thing — 1 point.

Pick the wrong team → **0 points** regardless of method.

## Streak counter 🔥

The main table shows a **STREAK** column — how many matches in a row you've scored on. It kicks in from **2 in a row** (below that you get a dash).

The Hall of Fame at the bottom shows the **best-ever streak** each player has managed — Tom Broughton is currently top with an 8-game run from the group stage.

## Your standing

Your own row is highlighted with a green stripe. The header shows your **rank / total players** and your **points tally**.

## Where does the data come from?

Results sync automatically every 30 minutes from football-data.org. Once a match is final, points settle immediately — no need to refresh.
