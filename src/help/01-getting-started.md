# Getting started

Welcome to the Wanstead Fellas app — the home for everything Thursday night.

## What the app does

It runs the whole Thursday-night operation:

- **Sign up** for the next game (or drop out if you can't make it)
- **See the teams** once they're published
- **Live scores** and goal scorers during the match
- **Vote for MOTM & DOTD** after full-time
- **See the match report** the next morning
- **Track your stats** — goals, MOTM wins, appearances, fines
- **Cup Predictor & Sweepstake** during World Cups

## The five main tabs

| Tab | What lives here |
|---|---|
| ⚽ **Next Game** | Sign up or drop out. Shows who's in, who's on the reserve list |
| 👥 **Teams** | The published team sheets for the next match |
| 📊 **Match** | Live scores tonight, or the last completed match's result + voting + report |
| 📈 **Stats** | Leaderboards — top scorers, MOTM winners, most fines, etc. |
| 🏆 **World Cup** | Only visible when a tournament's on — predictor + sweepstake |

The **⋯ More** tab (top-right when the Cup's on) or the **5th tab** otherwise holds History, Cards, Pods, Feedback, and — for admin — the Admin dashboard.

## First-time setup

1. Sign in with your email
2. Fill in your **Profile** — add a photo, pick your preferred position and foot, tell us your favourite club
3. Enable **notifications** so you get pinged when it's time to vote
4. Sign up for the next game and you're off

That's it. The rest of the app just works around you.
