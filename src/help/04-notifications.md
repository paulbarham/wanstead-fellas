# Notifications — set up

Get pinged when it's time to vote for MOTM & DOTD, and again when the results are in. Takes 30 seconds. One-time setup per device.

## What you'll get

| When | What |
|---|---|
| 🏆 After the final whistle | "Vote for tonight's awards" |
| 📊 Friday morning-ish | "MOTM & DOTD results published" |

That's it. No spam.

## iPhone / iPad

iPhones need a small extra step because iOS only allows notifications from apps you've added to the Home Screen.

1. **Open the app in Safari** — `wanstead-fellas.vercel.app`
2. Tap the **📤 Share button** at the bottom of Safari
3. Scroll and pick **"Add to Home Screen"** → tap **Add**
4. Now open the app **from the Home Screen icon** (the black-and-yellow WF badge)
5. Tap your **avatar** (top-right) → Profile
6. Scroll to the **🔔 Match-night notifications** card → tap **Enable notifications**
7. Tap **Allow** when your phone asks. Done.

## Android

No Home Screen faff needed — notifications work straight from Chrome.

1. Open the app in Chrome — `wanstead-fellas.vercel.app`
2. Tap your **avatar** (top-right) → Profile
3. Scroll to the **🔔 Match-night notifications** card → tap **Enable notifications**
4. Tap **Allow** when Chrome asks. Done.

## Turning them off

Same card → **Turn off on this device**. Same button to turn back on. Only affects the current device — if you've enabled on multiple phones, do each one.

## Something not working?

**"I tapped Enable and nothing happens"** — most common cause on iPhone: you're using the app in Safari, not the Home Screen app. Go back to step 1 above.

**"I tapped Block by mistake"** — Safari → Settings → Advanced → Website Data → find `wanstead-fellas.vercel.app` → re-enable. Or easier: uninstall the Home Screen icon and start again.

**"Nothing arrived on Thursday"** — check your Do Not Disturb / Focus modes aren't muting the app.
